Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Lumina: Daily Manifestation (“Lumina”) is built to be private. We don't collect, store or sell your personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter stays on your iPhone and, to show it there, your paired Apple Watch:

  • Your first name, if you choose to enter it
  • Your focus areas, card theme, reminder times and sound settings
  • Manifestations you write, favorites, your daily intention and streak
  • Mood check-ins
  • Voice recordings of your affirmations

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting Lumina deletes it.

Microphone

Lumina asks for microphone access only when you choose to record an affirmation in your own voice. Recordings are saved on your iPhone and never uploaded.

Apple Health

If you turn on “Save to Apple Health” in Settings, Lumina writes Mindful Minutes from breathing sessions and your mood check-ins to the Health app. Lumina never reads your health data, and health data is never used for advertising or shared with anyone.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. Their content is not sent through any server.

Purchases

Lumina Premium is sold through Apple. Apple processes your payment; we never see your payment details. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Lumina does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and post the new version on this page.
