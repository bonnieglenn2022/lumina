Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription or free trial?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Lumina, and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends to avoid the next charge. You can also tap Manage Subscription in Lumina's Settings.

I paid but Premium isn't unlocked

Open Lumina, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My reminders don't arrive

Check that notifications are on for Lumina in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Lumina, and that a reminder is switched on in Lumina's Settings. Focus modes can also hold notifications back.

How do I add Lumina to my Lock Screen or Home Screen?

Touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, tap the widget area and choose Lumina. For the Home Screen, touch and hold an empty spot, tap Edit → Add Widget, and search for Lumina.

How do I use Lumina on Apple Watch?

Install Lumina on your watch from the Watch app on your iPhone. To add it to a watch face, touch and hold the face, tap Edit, go to Complications and choose Lumina.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone and Apple Watch. See the Privacy Policy.
