Lumina Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Lumina ("the app") is designed to be private by default. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Lumina has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

Information stored on your device

Your first name (if you choose to enter it), focus areas, reminder times, favorites, streak, daily intention, and the manifestations you write are stored only on your iPhone and, to display them, on your paired Apple Watch. This information never leaves your devices and is not sent to us or any third party. Deleting the app deletes this information.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No notification content is sent to a server.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Lumina does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and post the new version at this address.
